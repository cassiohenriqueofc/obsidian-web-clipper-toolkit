## Scope assessment

The scope expanded from capture templates to project infrastructure because the
owner requested packaging, reuse, localization and memory rules. That is explicit
scope growth rather than an unrequested feature detour. Keep the generic starter
separate from templates and avoid further feature growth until live capture review.

## Recommended review

Use a strong reasoning-capable model for a short architecture/license review if
needed, then a lower-cost coding model at medium effort for concrete fixes.
Do not infer model prices from display names. Preserve unresolved facts in the
handoff; do not invent external advice or claim a model switch was performed.
